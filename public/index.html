⚙️ Algorithm Under the Hood
🔙
Backtracking
Core search: assigns digits recursively and undoes bad choices.
📡
Forward Checking
Prunes domains after each assignment to avoid exploring dead ends.
📉
MRV Heuristic
Always picks the letter with fewest remaining options — fail fast.
⚖️
LCV Heuristic
Tries the digit that eliminates the fewest options for others.
